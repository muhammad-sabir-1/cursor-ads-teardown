# Cursor ad teardown — July 2026

A read of every active ad on Cursor's Meta Ad Library page, plus 14 creatives pulled to show.

**Live sheet: https://muhammad-sabir-1.github.io/cursor-ads-teardown/**

Source: Meta Ad Library page `714727505056676`, country=ALL, active only, scraped 2026-07-30.
All 1,053 active ads were measured; the 14 on the page are a deliberate sample, not the top 14
by impressions.

## Headline numbers

| Cut | Numbers |
|-----|---------|
| Active ads | 1,053 |
| Media mix | 712 video (68%) · 341 image (32%) · 0 carousel |
| Source | 813 brand page (77%) · 240 creator partners (23%) |
| Days active | max 22d · avg 12.6d · median 11d |
| Unique concepts | 65 brand hooks + 31 creator hooks = 96, fanned out over 1,053 ads |

| Creator roster | 29 handles, median 7 ads each |

Three things worth the click:

1. **Nothing is older than 22 days.** Not one ad in 1,053. The account was relaunched around
   8 July 2026, so there is no survivorship signal in it yet.
2. **Two engines, two formats.** All 341 image ads come from the brand page. All 240 creator
   ads are video. The creator layer owns the top of the impression-sorted view but is under a
   quarter of the volume.
3. **~146 ads for the Grok 4.5 launch inside one week.** A launch burst running on top of an
   evergreen grid where the same 4 or 5 claims are rewritten many times over.
